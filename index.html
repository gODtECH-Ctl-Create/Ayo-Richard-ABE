WHAT I DO

I work where product meets engineering.

Not every problem needs more code. Sometimes it needs a clearer product decision, a better workflow, or a system that removes the boring parts.

01

Product development

Turn an idea into a working product by connecting the problem, user experience, product requirements, and implementation.

02

Product management

Shape priorities, define scope, organize delivery, and keep product decisions connected to the people the product is meant to serve.

03

Help developers reduce friction with internal tools, reusable workflows, automation, clearer architecture, and practical systems.
