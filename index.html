WHAT I DO

I work where product meets engineering.

Not every problem needs more code. Sometimes it needs a clearer product decision, a better workflow, or a system that removes the boring parts.

01

Product development

Turn an idea into a working product by connecting the problem, user experience, product requirements, and implementation.

02

Product management

Shape priorities, define scope, organize delivery, and keep product decisions connected to the people the product is meant to serve.

03

Developer systems

Help developers reduce friction with internal tools, reusable workflows, automation, clearer architecture, and practical systems.

04

Prototyping & automation

Build fast experiments that answer important questions before a team spends too much time or money going in the wrong direction.

FOR DEVELOPERS

Make the work easier to do.

I’m especially interested in the systems behind the software: the tools, automations, patterns, and internal workflows that help developers spend more time creating and less time fighting the process.

ABOUT

I don't just ask what to build.
I ask why it should exist.

I enjoy sitting in the middle of a problem: understanding what people need, translating that into a product, thinking through the system underneath it, and then helping get the thing shipped.

My work spans software development, product management, strategy, user experience, Artificial Intelligence (AI), automation, and the practical details that make products work in the real world.
